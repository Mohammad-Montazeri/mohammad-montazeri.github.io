About Me

I am an M.Sc. student in Mechanical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology and a research assistant in the Medical Robotics Laboratory, supervised by Prof. Saeed Behzadipour. My research combines robotics, biomechanics, and computer vision, with a focus on fine hand-motion estimation for telerehabilitation. I am developing a vision-based motion-capture pipeline and integrating it with biomechanical models to study physically feasible grasps for people with neurological impairments.

I received my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Tehran in 2025. At the Human and Robot Interaction Laboratory (TaarLab), supervised by Prof. Mehdi Tale Masouleh, I investigated deep-learning-based kinematics and redundancy resolution for bio-inspired Gough–Stewart robots. My related work explores optimization of multi-segment platforms for rehabilitation applications. Alongside research, I have served as a teaching assistant in engineering courses and taught robotics to high-school students.
